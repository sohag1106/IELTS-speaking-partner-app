Summary

Six changes carry most of the mobile gain

Everything above reduces to a short list. Two of these are layout, two are new controls, two are copy — and none of them touch the exam protocol, the server timer, or the auto-ready flow that already works.

Layout Give the examiner's video a fixed short strip on phones, and give the examinee's cue card the sticky slot during prep. The pinned video column stops being one rule for both roles.
Navigation One full-width primary in the lobby, everything else demoted. Three equal buttons is not a choice, it's a menu.
Timers One countdown strip, visible on every timed screen, in words as well as colour. The ring stays; it just stops being the only place the clock lives.
Controls A persistent dock for mic, camera, notes and leave. Round targets where the thumb already rests, instead of chips under the video.
Scoring Half-band chips instead of a select, the number named on the submit button, and the four criteria shown as prompts for recall.
Honesty Say who gave the score on the profile, say what a disconnect costs on the banner, say the camera prompt is coming before it arrives.
